GoHighLevel shows up constantly in the SMB and agency automation conversation — and for good reason, it's genuinely capable as a CRM-plus-automation platform. It's also easy to misconfigure in ways that don't show up until a workflow silently fails in production.

<OperatorNote>
The most common GHL failure mode we see: merge tag mismatches between a custom field name and what a workflow references. It fails silently — no error, the automation just doesn't fire. Test every workflow against a real contact record before trusting it in production.
</OperatorNote>

## Where GHL earns its place

- Consolidating CRM, pipeline, and marketing automation into one platform instead of three
- Custom field + workflow automation for lead routing and follow-up sequences
- Client-facing funnels and booking without a separate tool
