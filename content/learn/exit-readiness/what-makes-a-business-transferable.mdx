I sit across from sellers every week who assume a strong P&L is enough. It isn't. A national M&A advisory firm managing hundreds of millions in active exits sees the same pattern on repeat: the businesses that command a premium multiple are the ones where the owner could step away for a quarter and nothing would break.

<OperatorNote>
The fastest way to estimate owner-dependence: list every decision made last week that required you personally. If that list is long, that's your pre-diligence work — not your banker's.
</OperatorNote>

## The transferability checklist

1. **Documented, owned processes** — not tribal knowledge.
2. **Diversified customer concentration** — no single account that could sink the business if it left.
3. **Clean, reconciled financials** — three years, no surprises in due diligence.
4. **A second layer of management** — someone besides the founder who can run point.

Every point on this list is operational work, not financial engineering. That's exactly why it gets skipped.
