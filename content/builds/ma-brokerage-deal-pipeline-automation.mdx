A national M&A brokerage running an active referral partner program was tracking partner attribution and agreement status by hand - a process that got slower, not faster, as the partner list grew.

<OperatorNote>
The unlock wasn't a fancier CRM. It was a referral-code pattern (first initial + last name + last four of contact ID) that eliminated attribution collisions without adding a manual tagging step anyone had to remember to do.
</OperatorNote>

## What we built

- Automated referral-code generation the moment a partner reaches the onboarding stage - no manual tagging, no spreadsheet lookup
- A partner agreement that auto-populates with the partner's details and sends for e-signature the moment onboarding starts
- Pipeline-stage automation that advances a partner to active status the instant their agreement is signed, with zero manual follow-up

## Result

Referral attribution errors dropped to effectively zero, and the team stopped losing time reconciling who-gets-credit disputes at commission time.
