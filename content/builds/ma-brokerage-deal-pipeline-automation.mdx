A national M&A brokerage running dozens of active deals at a time was tracking referral attribution and deal-stage handoffs manually — a process that scaled linearly with headcount instead of leverage.

<OperatorNote>
The unlock wasn't a fancier CRM. It was a referral-code pattern (first initial + last name + last four of contact ID) that eliminated attribution collisions without adding a manual tagging step anyone had to remember to do.
</OperatorNote>

## What we built

- Automated referral-partner attribution at the point of lead capture, no manual tagging required
- Deal-stage workflows that trigger the right document package automatically as a deal progresses
- E-signature pipelines gated behind data-completeness checks — nothing goes out for signature until the underlying deal data is verified complete

## Result

Referral attribution errors dropped to effectively zero, and the team stopped losing time reconciling who-gets-credit disputes at commission time.
