Home services businesses — plumbing, HVAC, roofing, general contracting — share a common operational profile: high lead volume, thin office staff, and a founder who's usually still in the truck or on the phone with customers.

<OperatorNote>
The highest-leverage first automation for most home services businesses isn't marketing — it's lead response time. Every minute of delay on an inbound lead measurably drops close rate. That's the automation to build before anything else.
</OperatorNote>

## Where this industry sees the fastest ROI

- Automated lead response and routing (speed-to-lead is the single biggest lever)
- Job-status-to-invoice handoffs that don't require office staff to re-key data
- CRM hygiene that makes the business sellable — buyers discount home services roll-ups heavily for messy customer data
